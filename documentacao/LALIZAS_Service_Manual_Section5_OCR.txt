4, FINDING LEAKS

the inflatable lifejack ilon 1
4.1 Remove the inflator mechanism with the 14mm socket, using the torque wrench set to 3 N.M.
4.2 Using a mix of approx 500 parts water to one part liquid soap, apply the mix over the surface of the
inflated bladder and watch carefully for air bubbles forming. After leaks have been marked, soapy water
should then be rinsed off using clean fresh water. Alternatively, the inflated bladder can be submerged in
a tank of clean fresh water and observations made for a column of air bubbles appearing.

4.3 If the leak is found to be from any part of the bladder material or welded seam, the lifejacket should
be deemed as FAILED and not suitable for use.

4.4 If a leak is located from the inlet valve, replace the valve and then retest the lifejacket in accordance
with the method for that bladder type.

NOTE: A record should be made of all lifejacket tested including date, serial number, PASS or FAIL (if
failed include a note of failure) and name of the Inspector. If the lifejacket is deemed to have failed,
inform the customer and advise the lifejacket is no longer suitable for use.

4.5 Lifejackets deemed to have passed Inspection & Pressure Test should be dried off and are then ready
for re-arming and packing.

4.11 After immersion it in salty water, it is recommended that the whole lifejacket is washed. Always
remove the auto-firing head before washing automatic inflated lifejackets.

Automatic systems have a manual system as well as a water activation unit. Before the automatic unit is
checked, first read and understand the manual servicing instruction at section 5.1.

To check the automatic system Lalizas, first inspect the inflator cap making sure the green “ready for
action” indicator is visible in the base of the unit.

Next remove and inspect the seal of the CO, cylinder. If the cylinder seal has bean pierced or the “red
fired” indicator is visible in the base of the unit the complete water activated device will need replacing.

To replace the water activated device, unscrew the inflator cap in an anticlockwise direction and discard
the used water activated device. Then install a new water activated device in the “inflation body” and
screw on the inflator cap in a clockwise direction until no thread is visible. Then check that the green
“ready for action” indicator it appears in the base.

Check the manual system “green tag” is in place and refit the CO, cylinder.

The replacing cylinder must be for:

e The adult lifejackets SOLAS Lamda 150N, SOLAS Delta, ISO 12402 Alpha, ISO 12402 Sigma, ISO
12402 Delta & ISO 12402 Ypsilon: 33gr cylinder.

© . The children’s lifejackets ISO 12402 Alpha: 22gr cylinder.
e The lifejackets SOLAS Lamda 275N & ISO 12402 OMEGA 275 N: 60gr cylinder.

NOTE: LALIZAS S.A. recommends that in every annual inspection of the lifejacket, the water
activated device (code 00340) should be replaced.
